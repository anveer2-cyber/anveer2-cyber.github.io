Our Clinics

Precise diagnosis and cause-specific treatment of vertigo and related pain.

NEW

Head & Neck Cone Beam CT
— Genoray Papaya

Sangkwaehan ENT Clinic has introduced a dedicated head & neck cone beam CT (Genoray Papaya), bringing 3D imaging that once required a university hospital visit right into the clinic.

High-resolution 3D images of the sinuses, temporal bone and facial skeleton are taken on site in minutes, with a low radiation dose compared to conventional CT — so diagnosis and treatment planning can happen in a single visit.

  • Sinusitis — accurate assessment of all sinus cavities
  • Chronic ear disease — middle ear & temporal bone imaging
  • Nasal bone fracture evaluation
  • Low-dose, fast, same-day scanning in the clinic
  • Immediate review with your doctor at the same visit

For International Patients

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you see international patients?

Yes. International residents and visitors in the Jinhae / Changwon area are welcome. Patients enrolled in Korean National Health Insurance (NHIS) are covered as usual; self-pay is also available for those without Korean insurance, and we can issue receipts and medical documentation for private or overseas insurance claims.

Do I need an appointment?

No — the clinic runs on a walk-in basis during business hours. If you have questions before visiting, please call +82-55-547-3550.

What if I don't speak Korean?

Our staff will do their best to assist you, and translation apps work well for medical consultations. You are also welcome to bring a Korean-speaking friend or interpreter.

What conditions do you treat most?

Dizziness and vertigo (BPPV, Ménière's disease, vestibular neuritis), hearing loss, tinnitus, hearing-aid prescription and fitting, sinusitis, allergic rhinitis (including immunotherapy), sore throat and tonsillitis, ear infections, snoring, and general ENT complaints for both adults and children.

Can I get imaging done on the same day?

Yes. Hearing tests, balance (vestibular) testing, head & neck ultrasound, ENT endoscopy and our new head & neck cone beam CT (Genoray Papaya) are all performed in the clinic, so in most cases diagnosis and treatment planning are completed in a single visit.
